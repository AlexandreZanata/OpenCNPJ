# Round 09 expansion: 50,000 commercial research questions

Date: 2026-11-08. Initial market: Mato Grosso (MT). This is the next AI's work
order; round 09 is not yet authored or executed.

## Outcome and authenticity

Create exactly 50,000 useful pt-BR questions/turns and apply every item to the
interpreter and actual declared data. These deterministic evaluation iterations
are not statistical model training. Human product validation stays deferred to
refinement. Do not open or run the existing H-blind set.

Distinguish publicly observed questions with retrievable provenance and reuse
rights from synthetic plausible business scenarios. Never invent customers,
interviews, quotes or validation by entrepreneurs. A documented regional word
does not make an authored commercial question a collected question. If reusable
real questions are unavailable, meet the target with explicitly synthetic
scenarios and disclose the provenance mix. No human recruitment/approval pause
is needed for this synthetic round.

## Business discovery and correlations

Assign a concrete business decision to each scenario. Cover wholesale targeting,
accounting services, maintenance, logistics, food supply, expansion planning,
territory allocation, root-level deduplication, stale contacts, business continuity
and complementary sectors. Potential prospects are not proven buyers or intent.
Declared CNAE is not actual procurement, crop/fleet ownership or a supplier link.
Registration is not physical opening; latest status date is not full history.

Explore complementary-sector ratios; registrations versus closures; capital
distributions versus declared size; age versus current status; branch penetration
versus population; concentration versus stock change; CNAE spatial co-occurrence;
and contrasting retail/wholesale/service mixes. Add unrelated-sector controls.
Low registered density alone does not establish unmet demand or profitability.

Independently tag current native operation, derivable from current artifacts,
new analytical operation required, external source required, ambiguous, or
unavailable. Desired features must not be forced into unsupported existing IR.
Store a business plan/capability requirement outside gold.ir when necessary.

For every analytical request define observation unit, geography, period,
numerator/denominator, missing/zero handling, ties, sample N and uncertainty.
Correlation requires paired observations, not two national totals. If implemented,
document method/coefficient, minimum sample, outliers, population effects,
multiple comparisons and possible Simpson reversals. Association is not causation,
a commercial relationship or proof of demand. Never fabricate coefficients.

External candidates include IBGE population, CEMPRE, PAM/PPM and official municipal
statistics. First verify actual availability, licence, competence, granularity,
join keys and compatibility. CEMPRE describes formal organizations/local units;
it does not establish a particular CNPJ's revenue, staff or purchases:
https://www.ibge.gov.br/estatisticas/economicas/comercio/9016-cadastro-central-de-empresas.html
Do not claim candidate sources are integrated. No paid services. Capital is not
revenue; contact presence is not permission to contact. Preserve existing privacy
policy; do not create personal-partner prospect lists.

## Coverage and gold-label protocol

Cross families with root/establishment unit, main/secondary activity, status,
MEI/Simples, porte, legal nature, capital, contacts, dates, geography, exclusions,
sort/pagination and dialogue actions. Use feasible pair/triple interaction
coverage; publish uncovered combinations. Finite samples cannot prove all wording.

Include entity/command collisions, abbreviations, homonyms, unknown names,
negation, same-message self-corrections, contradictions, exact numeric boundaries,
Brazilian punctuation, malformed dates, alphanumeric CNPJ where supported,
archive availability, start-date versus closure-date constraints, missing CNAEs,
multiple activities and double-counting traps. Never exclude hard municipalities.

Add qualified metamorphic checks: accents/case and filler alone preserve intent;
explicit negation changes its target; narrower filters cannot add members on the
same artifact; exclusions remove members; independent dialogues do not share
state. Do not assert monotonicity across different snapshots, rankings or units.

Use eval-compatible id, text, gold, rules, meta, context. Extend meta with business
decision, primary family, geographic stratum/state, register, regional evidence
IDs, scenario-family ID, novelty reason, capability requirement, provenance class,
source references, label rationale/status and split. No fake or empty source URLs.
Synthetic and unverified linguistic hypotheses must remain visibly labelled.

Use globally unique round-prefixed sessions. Turns count toward 50,000; a full
conversation stays in one split. Allocate 35,000 synthetic development, 10,000
synthetic evaluation and 5,000 synthetic challenge items. Split by semantic plan,
scenario/template family and session, not random rows. Paraphrases, filler edits
and slot substitutions must not leak across splits. Report unavoidable follow-up
text reuse with context. These splits are not H-blind or human validation.

Author meanings/labels before seeing predictions. Review every label in a
separate pass/context against source rules, catalogue IDs, calendar arithmetic
and an explicit filter inventory. The same AI in both roles is not independent
human annotation. Do not seed predictions/state from gold.ir or previous_ir.
Record suspected label changes in hash-bound adjudication overlays; preserve
frozen originals. Distinguish old catalogue/representation differences from
genuine interpretation failures.
